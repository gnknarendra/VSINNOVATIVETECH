SAP FICO Syllabus

1. Introduction to SAP S/4 HANA and ERP
  • ERP concepts and SAP landscape
  • SAP ECC vs S/4 HANA: key simplifications
  • SAP Fiori launchpad and navigation
  • Universal Journal (ACDOCA) concept
2. Enterprise Structure
  • Client, company, company code, business area
  • Controlling area, operating concern, credit control area
  • Fiscal year variant, posting period variant, field status variant
  • Chart of accounts, account groups and number ranges
3. FI - General Ledger Accounting
  • Global parameters and company code settings
  • GL master data (chart of accounts and company code segments)
  • Ledgers, leading and non-leading ledgers, currency types
  • Document types, posting keys, document splitting
  • Parking, holding, reversing and recurring documents
  • Tolerance groups and automatic account assignment
  • Foreign currency valuation and exchange rates
4. FI - Accounts Payable (Vendor Accounting)
  • Business Partner (vendor) master data in S/4 HANA
  • Vendor invoices, credit memos and down payments
  • Automatic payment program (F110)
  • Withholding tax (TDS) configuration and posting
  • Special GL transactions and reconciliation accounts
5. FI - Accounts Receivable (Customer Accounting)
  • Business Partner (customer) master data
  • Customer invoices, incoming payments and clearing
  • Dunning, interest calculation and credit management
  • Down payment requests and bill of exchange
6. FI - Bank Accounting
  • House banks and bank accounts
  • Manual and electronic bank statement
  • Check management and cash journal
  • Bank reconciliation
7. FI - Asset Accounting (FI-AA)
  • Chart of depreciation, depreciation areas and asset classes
  • Asset master data, acquisition and retirement
  • Depreciation key, run and asset transfers
  • Asset under construction and settlement
  • Asset Accounting changes in S/4 HANA
8. Period-End and Year-End Closing
  • Opening and closing posting periods
  • Accruals, provisions and GR/IR clearing
  • Foreign currency valuation and balance carry forward
  • Financial statement versions and reports
9. CO - Controlling
  • Controlling area settings and cost element accounting
  • Cost centers, cost center groups and standard hierarchy
  • Activity types, statistical key figures, allocations (assessment and distribution)
  • Internal orders: planning, settlement and reports
  • Profit center accounting
  • Profitability Analysis (CO-PA): account-based and costing-based
  • Product costing basics and cost center planning
10. Integration with MM, SD and Other Modules
  • MM-FI integration: goods receipt, invoice verification, account determination
  • SD-FI integration: billing and revenue account determination
  • Material valuation and GR/IR
11. Live Project and Interview Preparation
  • End-to-end real-time project walkthrough
  • Support project (ticket) handling and common issues
  • Resume preparation and mock interviews
  • FICO interview questions and answers
